How It Works

Every Monday at 6 AM Eastern, the system automatically discovers apartment complexes via Google Places, analyzes their reviews for maintenance complaints, checks county permit databases, looks up property appraiser records for building age, and cross-references Florida Sunbiz for corporate ownership. Each property gets a 0-100 opportunity score.
